Every dev blog starts somewhere. This is mine.

## Why a blog?

Writing things down forces me to actually understand them. If I can't explain it clearly, I don't really get it yet. That's the main reason.

The other reason: this stuff is hard. There's always some Stack Overflow thread or random GitHub issue that saves the day, written by someone who ran into the same problem. I want to be that person sometimes.

## What I'll write about

Mostly whatever I'm learning or building. That usually means:

- **CS coursework** — algorithms, systems, software engineering concepts
- **Projects** — things I'm building, how they work, what broke and why
- **Tools and workflows** — editors, terminal setups, things that make life easier
- **Random tech thoughts** — things I find interesting

Not every post will be deep. Sometimes it'll just be "here's the thing that fixed my problem."
